The four systems

Four bottlenecks. One operations team that runs while you sleep.

Each system is custom-built and plugged directly into how your business already runs. Together, they replace a department.

01 / Voice Reception

The receptionist that never calls in sick.

When your office closes, your competitors stay open. Our AI voice receptionist answers every call, day or night, in a natural human voice. Callers don't get voicemail purgatory — they get answers, bookings, and the feeling that you're paying attention even when you're not.

What it does
Answers calls 24/7 in a voice indistinguishable from a real receptionist
Books appointments directly into your calendar
Qualifies leads and routes urgent calls to your mobile
Captures every detail and emails a clean summary before you wake up

The result: You stop losing the 40% of business that happens outside business hours.

02 / Lead Generation

Your pipeline should be a firehose, not a drip.

Cold lists are dead. Spray-and-pray prospecting is dead. What works now is precision — knowing exactly who to target, when to reach them, and what to say. We build lead engines tailored to your exact ideal customer profile, no matter your industry.

What it does
Identifies and enriches thousands of prospects matching your ICP
Pulls verified contact data, intent signals, and personalization triggers
Delivers ready-to-contact leads directly into your CRM, refreshed continuously

The result: You stop wondering where your next customer is coming from.

03 / Inbox Zero EA

Open your inbox. See zero. Every single morning.

Email is where focus goes to die. Our Inbox Zero Executive Assistant doesn't just filter spam — it acts like a real chief of staff inside your inbox. It reads every email, drafts replies in your voice, schedules meetings, files what doesn't need a response, and surfaces only what genuinely needs your attention.

What it does
Triages every incoming email and categorizes by urgency
Drafts reply suggestions trained on your past writing style
Books meetings without back-and-forth and summarizes long threads
Flags only what truly needs your judgment

The result: You stop being a slave to your inbox and start running your day on your own terms.

04 / Outbound Email

Personalized outreach to 10,000 — without sending one yourself.

Mass email is dead. Personalization at scale is the future, and AI is the only way to do it without an army of SDRs. Our outbound system writes individually personalized messages, references each prospect's actual business and recent activity, follows up across multiple touches, and adjusts tone based on responses.

What it does
Researches each prospect individually using real-time signals
Writes unique emails referencing their company, role, and pain points
Manages multi-touch sequences with smart timing
Hands hot replies directly to your sales team

The result: You stop hiring SDRs and start booking meetings on autopilot.
