01 / Dental practice 5-person clinic Voice receptionist Live in 18 days

The clinic that stopped losing 60 calls a week. Booking rate jumped 34%.

The problem

A general-practice dental office with one front-desk lead was losing roughly sixty calls a week after hours and on weekends. New patients were calling competing practices the moment they hit voicemail.

"Voicemail is where new patients go to call somebody else."

What we built

The After-Hours Voice Receptionist, voiced to match their existing front-desk personality, with direct integration into the practice management system's calendar. Calls outside business hours route straight to the AI; in-hours overflow rings the front desk first, then falls back.

Urgent dental issues (pain, swelling, trauma) are escalated to the on-call dentist's mobile within ninety seconds.

The result

Zero missed after-hours calls in the first sixty days. Booking conversion on after-hours calls clocked in at 34% higher than the in-hours human-handled baseline because the AI books on the spot rather than promising a callback.

02 / Commercial roofing 12-person crew Lead generation Live in 24 days

Replaced a six-figure SDR burn with a precision pipeline. Eight months no cold outreach.

The problem

A regional commercial roofing operator had been burning roughly $180K a year on outsourced SDRs running cold lists. Conversion was thin, the lists were stale by the time they got reached, and the owner was personally rebuilding the pipeline every quarter.

What we built

The Lead Generation engine, tuned to commercial-property triggers: roof-age signals from public permitting data, recent ownership changes, and weather-event correlations in their service region. Verified contact data is pulled per prospect and refreshed on a rolling weekly cycle into their CRM.

Hand-off rules route hot leads with intent signals to the owner; cooler leads drop into the outbound nurture sequence.

The result

Calendar booked four to six weeks out within the first quarter. SDR contract was paused at the 60-day mark and never restarted — the owner says he hasn't manually run cold outreach in eight months.

03 / Marketing agency 25-person agency Inbox Zero EA Live in 12 days

From 400 unread to actual zero, every morning. The CEO's mornings, returned.

The problem

A 25-person performance-marketing agency CEO was opening the inbox to four hundred unread messages every morning. The first two hours of every day disappeared into triage, and important client threads got buried under newsletters and scheduling back-and-forth.

What we built

The Inbox Zero Executive Assistant, trained on a year of the CEO's outbound messages so reply drafts read in her voice. Urgency triage runs on every incoming thread; meeting requests are auto-scheduled inside her availability windows; long client threads are summarized into bullet points.

The CEO's queue surfaces only the messages that genuinely need her judgment — usually six to ten a day.

The result

Inbox holds steady at zero by the time she sits down with coffee. She estimates she's reclaimed roughly fifteen hours a week, mostly from the front of the day, which she's pushing into product strategy and client calls.
